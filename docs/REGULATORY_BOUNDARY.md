# Regulatory Boundary

SCN is designed as infrastructure first.

A production deployment may trigger regulation depending on:
- custody;
- arranging deals;
- brokerage;
- exchange operation;
- lending;
- stablecoin issuance;
- securities/tokenized-security issuance;
- collective investment management;
- derivatives;
- money transmission/payment services;
- investment advice;
- client-asset handling.

Those functions should be separately gated and, where appropriate, performed by appropriately licensed entities or partners.

The technology company should not market a product as "Sharia-compliant" merely because a rules engine exists. A specific product requires an actual governance process, competent reviewers, documented scope, and ongoing review.
