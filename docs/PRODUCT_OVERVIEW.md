# Product Overview

## Problem

Tokenization does not itself make an asset trustworthy, liquid, enforceable, or financeable.

Institutional users need a machine-readable record of:
- identity;
- ownership/control;
- custody;
- evidence lineage;
- valuation sources;
- encumbrances;
- policy restrictions;
- concentration;
- liquidity;
- counterparty dependencies;
- governance status.

## Product modules

### 1. Asset Passport
Canonical record for an asset or financial claim.

### 2. Evidence Graph
Links assertions to source artifacts and records confidence / verification state.

### 3. Encumbrance Ledger
Tracks gross value, pledged value, liens/claims, financing references, and available value.

### 4. Collateral Registry
Public demo uses categorical states only:
`INELIGIBLE`, `REVIEW`, `ELIGIBLE_SYNTHETIC`.

### 5. Governance Console
Proposal, review, approval, timelock, execution, veto and emergency-state model.

### 6. Risk Graph
Models dependencies rather than pretending protocols or counterparties are isolated.

### 7. Settlement Simulator
Double-entry-style synthetic ledger that demonstrates mint/redeem mechanics without real assets.

## Intended institutional users

- banks;
- custodians;
- asset managers;
- tokenization platforms;
- commodity/trade-finance operators;
- Islamic-finance institutions;
- family offices;
- digital-asset infrastructure providers;
- auditors and risk teams.

## Initial commercial wedge

Institutional digital assets and tokenized real-world assets in the GCC, with an optional Sharia-governance rail layered over a neutral evidence/collateral core.
