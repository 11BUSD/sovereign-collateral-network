# Hydrocarbon Collateral — Public Product Boundary

SCN is being explored as a continuous collateral-assurance layer for GCC commodity finance, beginning with stored refined-product inventory and receivables. It is not a commodity exchange, terminal operator, warehouse registry, custodian, lender, title registry, token issuer, or legal determination engine.

## What SCN contributes

Existing systems and participants may establish or report physical facts, ownership records, warehouse warrants, inspection results, pledges, payments, and market data. SCN joins those inputs into a versioned institutional decision record:

1. What asset lot, receivable, usufruct, or contractual claim is being evaluated?
2. Which evidence supports quantity, quality, custody, title assertions, insurance, and provenance?
3. Which prior claims, pledges, allocations, restrictions, or negative pledges exist?
4. Which institution-specific policy applies?
5. What remains potentially financeable after encumbrance, eligibility, concentration, liquidity, and control checks?
6. Which exceptions require human approval?
7. What changed since the previous collateral certificate?

SCN records assertions and their provenance. It does not silently convert a sensor reading, database entry, document, blockchain record, or agent output into legal title, perfected security, regulatory permission, or Sharia approval.

## Recommended first pilot

The first design-partner pilot should use one refined product, one independently operated UAE terminal or storage pattern, one financier, and one synthetic or sanitized facility. The system should run in shadow mode and reproduce a daily collateral certificate and exception pack.

LNG cargo interests are a later expansion. They combine maritime title, measurement, boil-off, quality, insurance, sanctions, route, terminal, and long-term contract complexity and should not be the first live collateral object.

## Cargo View boundary

An evidence system such as Cargo View can report vessel, terminal, inspection, provenance, or movement events. SCN consumes those events as evidence. SCN separately manages asset passports, rights and claims, encumbrance, institutional eligibility, borrowing-base simulation, covenants, approvals, and audit history.

## Islamic-finance boundary

- Commodity inventory and working-capital flows may be relevant to Murabaha, Salam, or other structures, subject to actual legal and Sharia design.
- Ijara is naturally aligned with durable assets and usufruct—such as tanks, vessels, terminal equipment, or pipelines—not fuel that is consumed.
- Musharaka may fit genuine co-ownership or project participation, not a label applied to ordinary secured lending.
- A recorded review scope is not a fatwa or universal claim of Sharia compliance.

## Reference context

- [DMCC Tradeflow](https://dmcc.ae/ecosystems/financial-services/tradeflow) already provides commodity ownership records, electronic warehouse warrants, pledge workflows, and Islamic-finance facilities in the UAE.
- [SAMA's commodities-finance definition](https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/entiresection/2813) expressly addresses short-term lending against inventories or receivables of exchange-traded commodities, including crude oil.
- [ADGM spot-commodity guidance](https://www.adgm.com/legal-framework/guidance-and-policy-statements) addresses title, delivery, storage, custody, AML/CFT, sanctions, insurance, and third-party audit considerations.
